Architecture, with context

The square's existing north–south arrangement and landmark positions are retained. Individual monument models now distinguish roof counts, open galleries, platforms, courtyards and sacred water structures.

Estimated dimensions. This is an interpretive reconstruction, not a measured digital twin. Site coordinates, building heights and surrounding streets remain estimates. Fine religious figures are simplified; inscriptions and narrative relief sequences are not fabricated.

What informs the model

Your supplied photograph informs the material palette, roof depth and visual character. Heritage documentation informs the identities of the monuments and the difference between the three water structures.

  • Krishna Mandir: three stone storeys, arched galleries, subsidiary stone pavilions and 21 gilded pinnacles. Pinnacle placement is a visual interpretation.
  • Chyasin Dega: a separate eight-sided stone structure, with its own platform and arcade.
  • Bhimsen, Vishwanath, Char Narayan and Hari Shankar: individually dimensioned three-, two-, two- and three-roof models.
  • Keshav Narayan, Mul and Sundari Chowk: genuine open courts within detailed palace wings, with western entrance passages.
  • Manga Hiti: a recessed cruciform public basin with three spouts. Tusha Hiti: a smaller oval, slightly cusped ritual bath inside Sundari Chowk. Bhandarkhal: the rear rectangular tank.
